# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository is a technical handbook with a public contribution trail.
Discussions should make the handbook better and the community worth
returning to. We expect:

- Criticism of code, explanations, and ideas: directed at the artifact,
  never the person.
- Technical disagreement resolved by evidence: benchmarks, release notes,
  reproducible examples.
- Patience with learners. Every chapter assumes a reader meeting the topic
  for the first time; answer questions at that level.
- Original work. Do not contribute copied text, and do not present other
  people's code or writing as your own.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: personal attacks, trolling or insult
trades, harassment of any kind, publishing others' private information,
bad-faith arguments sustained after correction, and spam.

## Scope

This standard applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, code
review), its spaces (discussions, chat channels, email threads about the
project), and anywhere a contributor is acting as a representative of the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce this standard. They may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Enforcement decisions
state the reason and are final.

## Reporting

Report violations by opening a private contact via the maintainer's GitHub
profile (<https://github.com/TharunKumarReddyPolu>) or the project website
(<https://tharunpolu.com>). Include links and screenshots where possible.
Reports are handled confidentially; reporters' identities are not disclosed
without their consent.
